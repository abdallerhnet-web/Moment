Turn your world into a game

  1. 1

    Capture your world

    Take a photo of your surroundings. It sets the colours and mood of your night.

  2. 2

    Connect your location

    GPS picks the real streets and buildings around you. Or type coordinates.

  3. 3

    Build and drive

    Roads and buildings are rebuilt in 3D, then you get the wheel.



Controls: drag on the left half to steer, hold GAS and BRAKE on the right. Keyboard: arrows or WASD. Your photo never leaves this device. Without GPS or when map data cannot load, a generated city seeded by your photo is used and the game says so.
